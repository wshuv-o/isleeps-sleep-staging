Highlights

- One model stages sleep and detects respiratory events in post-stroke PSG
- Aligning cross-fitted embeddings unlocks a sleep-adapted EEG encoder downstream
- 76.3% staging accuracy and 0.825 respiratory AUC on 99 ischemic stroke patients
- Ahead of thirteen retrained staging baselines under patient-independent folds
- Respiratory detection transfers to two external cohorts at 0.83-0.84 AUC
